Biography

Mohamed Naziq Sabeeulla is a doctoral student in the Department of Agricultural and Biological Engineering at Mississippi State University. He holds an M.Sc. in Agricultural Meteorology from the Agro Climate Research Center, Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU), where he coupled crop simulation modeling with high-resolution weather forecasts and sensor-based soil moisture data to develop a forecast-driven irrigation scheduling framework for maize. Before joining MSU, he served as a Senior Research Fellow at ICAR-IARI, New Delhi, working on crop modeling and remote sensing for operational yield forecasting across different crops.

His current research applies process-based crop simulation to furrow-irrigated cotton in the Mississippi Delta, combining field-scale soil water measurements, cover crop management, and long-term simulation to evaluate irrigation strategies that address alluvial aquifer depletion. His research interests include crop simulation modeling, irrigation decision support systems, soil–water dynamics, agricultural water management, and remote sensing and GIS.
